# Worked example: ShammyVerse (November 2026)

ShammyVerse: five dolphins escort the candy ship and leap with splashes (`open_sea_life.gd`), three striped ships form a candy armada and gulls trail the stern (`voyage_life.gd`, `sea_extras.gd`). The music-video adapter calls the escort in every sailing shot.
