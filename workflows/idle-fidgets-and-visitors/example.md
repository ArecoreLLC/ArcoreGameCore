# Worked example: ShammyVerse (November 2026)

ShammyVerse: Poofy looks around, stretches his wings, swishes his tail, sniffs and yawns after 3.5 s still; after 6 s a butterfly lands on his snout, a bubble pops on his nose (he sneezes), a gull lands and stares, or a crab scuttles past (`poofy_reactions.gd`, `close_visitors.gd`).

The game code stays in the ShammyVerse repository (`game/scripts/`, `Design/Iterations/world-life-v001/DESIGN_LOG.md`).
The GameCore component was generalised from it and checked by the headless component test.
