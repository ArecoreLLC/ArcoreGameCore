Expanded Gameplay, Mechanics & Graphics DirectionTo bring these 28 games (and the 4 flagship combinations) to life, here is an expanded creative and visual direction tailored to each engine's strengths—leveraging Unreal 5.8.4's lighting, Chaos physics, and procedural geometry recipes alongside Godot 4.7.2's expressive 3-tone stylized toon shaders (ShammyVerse lineage) and Phaser/React's crisp tactile UI.Wave 1: Reusable Foundations (Priorities 1–4)1. lantern-heist — Lantern Heist (Unreal / C++)Expanded Gameplay Loop: You play as a pint-sized clockwork thief infiltrating a three-room nocturnal museum (Rotunda, Archive, Vault). You carry a shuttered amber lantern: opening the shutter reveals hidden pressure plates and charges light-sensitive door prisms (Lumen-Weaver donor), but casts a real-time light cone that alerts the Custodian automaton. Dropping brass decoys or stepping on creaky floorboards versus plush rugs (cap-surface-footsteps) lets you manipulate the guard's investigation routes while you reroute light bridges to extract the Relic.Graphics & Visual Identity: Chiaroscuro Miniature Diorama. Isometric cutaway camera with smooth obstruction fade (cap-camera-obstruction-recovery). Deep indigo shadows contrasted against warm, volumetric amber lantern beams and dust motes. Characters are stylized brass-and-porcelain automata. Sound waves emit subtle concentric floor ripples (color-coded by pitch/pattern for muted/accessible play).2. clockwork-courier — Clockwork Courier (Unreal / C++)Expanded Gameplay Loop: Before each run on a rugged canyon circuit, you snap together a chassis, wheel size, suspension springs, and cargo rack (Snap-Rally donor). Your cargo—a crate of unstable alchemical vials—shifts weight dynamically in the rack. Braking too hard on a downhill hairpin or bottoming out your suspension shatters vials, reducing your payout and lightening the load.Graphics & Visual Identity: Tactile Kitbash Off-Road. Sun-baked terracotta canyon with visible tire ruts, kicking pebble particles, and exaggerated spring compression/extension visuals (springs glow orange as they approach max load). Ghost trial vehicles render as translucent blueprint wireframes with trailing ribbon splines showing braking zones.3. pocket-expedition — Pocket Expedition (Unreal / C++)Expanded Gameplay Loop: Before descending into a 5-room shifting ruin, you pack a grid-based knapsack (cap-inventory-capacity) where item adjacency matters (e.g., placing glowing moss next to glass flasks pre-brews a flash-salve). Inside, rooms connect via weighted stone doors. The two-phase Guardian starts as an armored sentinel (requiring crafted acid or flanking) before shedding its shell in Phase 2 (cap-boss-phase-transitions) to become a fast, aggro-hunting stalker you must lure into ancient traps to escape.Graphics & Visual Identity: Mossy Relic Stone & Parchment. High-contrast dungeon lighting where your backpack physically hangs on the character's back and visibly displays packed items on exterior loops. UI uses diegetic parchment fold-outs with stamped ink icons.4. garden-council — Garden Council (Phaser / TypeScript)Expanded Gameplay Loop: Three specialist gardeners (Lifter, Pruner, Water-Bearer) navigate a flooded 8x8 conservatory grid to rescue trapped sproutlings before frost sets in. Each turn, you play from a 12-card deck (cap-deck-construction) where cards stack reactions (cap-card-effect-stack)—e.g., playing Divert Stream pushes a floating log, which triggers a queued Vine Grapple to pull two gardeners across at the cost of 1 Action Point.Graphics & Visual Identity: Botanical Woodblock & Stained Glass. Crisp 2D vector-art tiles with distinct silhouette patterns on every hazard and buff (cap-color-independent-signals). Playing cards feature embossed botanical illustrations that animate into paper-cut pop-ups when resolving on the stack.Wave 2: Combine Systems into Durable Loops (Priorities 5–12)5. foundry-friends — Foundry Friends (Unreal / C++)Expanded Gameplay Loop: Cozy factory management where round forest critters power conveyor splitters, stampers, and parcel wrappers (Critter-Contraptions donor). Warm brass gears, canvas conveyor belts with stitched seams, and chunky mechanical toggle switches that flip with satisfying sparks and steam puffs.6. storm-harbor — Storm Harbor (Unreal / C++)Expanded Gameplay Loop: Piloting a twin-compartment salvage trawler across a treacherous strait (Flotsam-Citadel & Tugboat-Tumble donors).
